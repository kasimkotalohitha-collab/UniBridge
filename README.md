 **UniBridge**

UniBridge is a unified campus platform that connects **students, faculty, wardens, and administrators** in one place to report, manage, track, and resolve campus issues. It combines role-based access, transparent complaint tracking, notifications, and AI-assisted complaint analysis.

**Live UniBridge**

 [Open UniBridge →](https://1da13858.unibridge-5fd.pages.dev/)

**Roles**

Student
Students can raise campus complaints, submit class and academic queries, upload supporting files, report anonymously when needed, track complaint progress, and receive notifications when their issues are updated.

Faculty & Wardens
Faculty and wardens can view assigned complaints and queries, update their progress, add remarks, and manage issues until they are resolved.

Administrator
Administrators can manage campus-wide complaints, assign departments and staff, control priorities, monitor progress, and view campus analytics.

AI-Powered Triage
Google Gemini analyzes complaints and assists with **category prediction, priority detection, department suggestions, summaries, and confidence scores**.
